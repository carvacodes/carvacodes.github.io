Upright Piano, Unbranded

This model of an upright piano is not based strictly on any existing instrument, but contains elements in several styles: Kawai and Yamaha (the gentle flare of the outer two pedals, the tapered profile of the music stand) and Steinway (the profile of the side board on either side of the keybed). Without a hinged top board (the upper-most lid) or a rear soundboard (the normally-exposed rear wood of a conventional upright), this model mimics a higher-end digital piano more closely than an acoustic upright. The heavily-rounded profile of the keybed's black keys and the rounded-geometric shape of the pedals are entirely unique.

The model features a minimal rig with rotational constraints for the fall board (the lid over the keys) and the music stand inside the fall board.

Most of the model's textures are vanilla Principled BSDF shaders with albedo, roughness, and metallicity settings approximating brass, lacquered wood, ivory, or ebony. The music stand and fall board hinges are textured using a color-ramped Brick Texture into Color Mix and Bump nodes to achieve the appearance of a butt hinge using minimal geometry.

The scene is lighted with a neutral color, overhead-lit HDRI, with additional lighting from an in-scene diffuser reflecting several neutral-white area lights. The diffuser itself is excluded from final renders.
